# Pricing rules

- The subtotal is the sum of each item's `price` times its `qty`.
- `SAVE10` takes 10% off the subtotal, rounded to the nearest cent, **only when the subtotal is at least $100.00**. Below that, the code is accepted but gives no discount. Finance set this minimum to protect margins on small orders, and any change needs their sign-off.
- Unknown codes give no discount.
